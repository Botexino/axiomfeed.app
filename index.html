§03 FAQ

Before you wire it up

The stuff everyone asks first.

How fast does it actually buy? +
Median fill is 0.38 seconds after the tweet goes live. On a good slot, alerts fire within 55ms — from there the only thing left is Solana block time.
Do I hand over my private keys? +
No. Axiom's non-custodial session signing. You approve once with spend caps and an expiry you set — keys never leave your wallet.
Which chains does it support? +
Solana today — that's where the memecoin flow lives. More chains on the roadmap, held to the same latency bar.
What if a tweet has no contract address? +
Nothing fires. Only acts when it can parse a valid CA. Pure hype with no address gets logged as an alert, never a buy.
Can I cap how much it spends? +
Yes — per tweet, per day, and a slippage ceiling. Once any limit is hit it stops buying and just alerts until you reset.
Is it safe to leave running 24/7? +
That's the point. Only acts inside the rules and caps you set, and session permissions expire on your timer — a forgotten tracker can't quietly drain a wallet.
